Room — / A note on the archiveThe living archive

Behind the pages.

This archive connects exhibitions, writing and professional history. Its empty frames reserve space for photographs that have not yet been supplied; no substitute artwork is used.

A readable, open structure

Type is set in Instrument Serif and DM Sans, available under the SIL Open Font License. Fonts are served with the site. Standard pages work without JavaScript; the archive controls and weekly anagram add browser interaction.

Sources & authorship

Project records reference institutional sources. Writing published elsewhere links to the original publication. Demonstration writing, when enabled for a review edition, is explicitly identified and is not attributed to Concetta.

Privacy & play

There are no analytics, advertising trackers or accounts on this site. The weekly anagram saves progress only in your browser. Use “Start again” to clear the current week’s saved game. If browser storage is unavailable, you can still play for the current visit.

External publications open on other websites with their own privacy practices. The hosting provider may maintain normal service logs.

Access

Use Tab to move between links and controls. The anagram accepts keyboard typing and letter-button input. Motion follows your system’s reduced-motion preference. The chronological résumé is also available as a simple printable page.
