Concetta Luise is an independent Italian art historian, writer and contemporary art curator. Her practice brings together exhibitions, research and writing, with a particular interest in exchanges between Italian and American contemporary art contexts.

Her experience spans curatorial work at The Drawing Center in New York and curatorial coordination at Whitespace Projects Napoli. Writing, collaboration and research run through the archive.
