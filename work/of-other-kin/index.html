Of Other Kin

Four artists consider the relationships between human and other forms of life, through fantasy and humor.

Dates
19 October — 11 November 2023
Institution / venue
Elza Kayal Gallery
Place
New York, United States
Role
Curator
Artists
Timothy Bair, Pearl Cowan, Hayley Youngs, Xiangni Song
Threads
KinshipPaintingNew York

Curated by Concetta Luise, Of Other Kin brought together Timothy Bair, Pearl Cowan, Hayley Youngs and Xiangni Song at Elza Kayal Gallery in New York.

The exhibition considers relationships between humans and other living beings. Fantasy and humor offer ways to question where one form of life ends and another begins.
