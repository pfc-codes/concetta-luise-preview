This Is Not All There Is was presented at The Drawing Center in New York from 2 June to 10 September 2023.

Concetta Luise contributed while working within the institution’s Curatorial Department. The exhibition was organized by Chief Curator Claire Gilman with Curatorial Associate Isabella Kapur.

The museum’s project page provides the exhibition description and complete institutional context.

Concetta’s contribution is documented in the Whitespace biography. The exhibition was organized by Claire Gilman with Isabella Kapur.
