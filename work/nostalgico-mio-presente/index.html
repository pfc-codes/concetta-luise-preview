Nostalgico mio presente

Matteo Silverii. Painting, memory and the experience of time, inside Naples’ ancient aqueduct.

Dates

Institution / venue

MemoryPaintingNaples

An exhibition of work by Matteo Silverii, curated by Concetta Luise at the Acquedotto Augusteo del Serino in Naples, in collaboration with Associazione VerginiSanità.

The project brings painting into a place shaped by historical time. Its title places nostalgia in the present: an invitation to consider how recollection and immediate experience meet in the image.
